I am a junior student at Tsinghua University, currently conducting research in the field of Computer Science. My areas of interest lies in Human Computer Interaction and Computer Vision, including: wearable device authentication, social computing, data visualization in VR as well as 3D generation by diffusion model. I hope to make continuous progress through learning and practical experience.

With a passion for outdoor activities, I enjoy cycling and hiking. I currently represent Tsinghua University as a member of the cycling team, and you can find my Strava account [here](https://www.strava.com/athletes/107292471). Feel free to reach out if you'd like to go for a ride together!

I'm also a soccer enthusiast, both as a player and a fan, and a dedicated supporter of Cristiano Ronaldo. In addition to sports, I have a deep appreciation for music, with a special fondness for Jay Chou and Steve Chou's songs.

That's a little bit about me – looking forward to connecting with like-minded individuals and making new friends!
