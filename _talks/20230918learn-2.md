# Today's Paper Presentation:AI Chains-Transparent and Controllable Human-AI Interaction by Chaining LLM Prompts

This paper discusses the insights of a researcher on the application of large language models (LLMs) in the field of artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on the challenges faced when assisting humans in complex tasks and the proposed novel solutions.

## Problem Description

- **Breadth of Scope**: LLMs have the ability to generate diverse text, but their versatility can sometimes lead to generated content that does not align with task requirements, as the generated text may lack specialization or relevance.
- **Lack of Transparency**: The internal workings of LLMs are often opaque, making it challenging to understand why they make specific decisions or generate particular text.
- **Insufficient Controllability**: LLMs' outputs are difficult to precisely control, making it challenging for users to guide the model in generating the content they need.

## Proposed Solutions

The researcher introduces the concept of "Chaining LLM steps together," which involves linking the operational steps of LLMs. This allows the output of one step to become the input for the next, accumulating improvements at each step. Key points include:

- **Definition of LLM Primitive Operations**: To construct Chains (sequential operations), a set of LLM primitive operations is defined, which can be combined to perform more complex tasks.
- **Interactive System**: To implement this concept, the researcher develops an interactive system that enables users to modularly modify these operational chains and their intermediate results, providing users with greater flexibility in utilizing LLMs to complete tasks. This significantly enhances the transparency, controllability, and sense of collaboration with LLMs.

Keywords for this paper include "Human-Machine Interaction," "Large Language Models," and "Natural Language Processing," highlighting the researcher's focus on improving interactions between humans and artificial intelligence systems, as well as research in the application of natural language processing techniques.
